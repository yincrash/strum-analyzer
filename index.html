Tips
  • Hold the phone or laptop mic a few cm from the belt and pluck it like a guitar string.
  • If the level meter never turns green, lower the Gate slider. If it is always green from room noise, raise it.
  • Clarity near 1.0 means a clean tone. Readings below 0.85 clarity are ignored.
  • 60 Hz mains hum has a harmonic at 120 Hz. If you see a steady 120 Hz with nothing plucked, that is hum, not the belt. Move away from power supplies or fans.
  • Presets come from each printer's public docs and are only starting points. Check your own manual, and note that the frequency depends on where you pluck and how long the free span is.
  • The FFT peak reading is a second opinion. If it disagrees by a factor of 2 the detector may be picking an octave; trust the one nearest the strongest spectrum bump.
